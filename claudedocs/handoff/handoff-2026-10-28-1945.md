## Decisions and why

- **Words that are not one token are excluded** from rule (b), and counted separately. This was
  the user's choice, after TinyStories at 1.50 split `river` and `seek`.
- **Matched controls are excluded from false flags** (user's choice). They concentrate the pattern
  where it is common: 46% of TinyStories' sentences.
- **Class false flags are read on whole-document controls only** (user's choice). Sentence cuts
  orphan closing quotes, and the class rows flag `."` and `!"`.
- **A recurring flag on one control counts once.** Otherwise FineWeb's `r25c` (` luck`) and `r25e`
  (`Maybe`) fail the pooled rule.
- **Prediction 3 of the 10-25 spec is judged on clustered tokens > 29,274**, not on whether the
  cell was skipped. It predicts SciPy's table, which still does not fit. The amendment is dated in
  the spec.
- **Robustness design:**
  - ¼ is read in all four corpora (cheap, and the realistic size of a domain corpus).
  - 4× is read only in TinyStories; elsewhere it needs `linkage_vector` and hours per cell.
  - The pools are nested across sizes and disjoint across draws.
  - **Rejected:** the full 4 corpora × 3 sizes × 11 values (132 cells, days of compute at 4×).
- **The experiments are not two independent validations.** SciPy and `linkage_vector` compute the
  same tree; `linkage_vector` only lowers the memory.

## Pitfalls and what did not work

- **Never more than 2 workers.** A memory-pressure kill takes the whole 16 GB machine down.
- **SciPy's Ward does not fit above about 29,274 clustered tokens** (F at 1.35).
- **Time estimates were up to 3× short,** for example Wikipedia 1.30's tree at 10 h against 3.4 h.
  Order the cheap cells first.
- **The shell is zsh: `$var` is not word-split.** Use `xargs -0`, or `${=var}`.
- **Background waits on a log must skip old lines.** One wait matched an earlier `DONE` line;
  scope the search with `sed -n '/marker/,$p'`.
- **Reassigning `domains.RESULTS` from another module broke paths.** Pass a `results` parameter
  instead.
